4. C-shaped line and row break

Incoming is at the top right; upper stations run left, then lower stations run right to Outgoing. Hover a gap to preview. Click a gap or drag the Row break marker to choose the turn between mapped physical stations.

Only mapped stations appear in the preview. Grouping keeps every process at its physical station; a row break cannot split one station.

5. Material movement proposal

The transport option follows one operator’s work order, keeping consecutive processes at the same station together. Incoming and Outgoing boxes are always included. Multi-operator handoffs need explicit Pick/Drop steps and buffers in Line Builder.

6. Review generated draft

    Opening a draft does not save or overwrite a scenario file. Line Builder asks before replacing unsaved work. Generated scenarios require a material-flow review before running; later edits require another check.
